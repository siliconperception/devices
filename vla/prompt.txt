The water cycle describes how water moves between the ocean, the atmosphere, and the land. Heat from the sun causes water at the surface of the ocean to evaporate, turning liquid water into invisible vapor that rises into the warm air. As the vapor cools, it condenses into tiny droplets of water that gather to form clouds. When the droplets grow heavy enough, they fall back to the ground as rain or snow.
